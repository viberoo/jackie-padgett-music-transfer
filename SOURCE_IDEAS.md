## Chosen Approach: Warm Americana Roadhouse

**Design Movement:** Rustic Americana / Southern Gothic warmth

**Core Principles:**
1. Warmth over polish — amber, mahogany, and candlelight tones create emotional intimacy
2. Asymmetric layouts with editorial rhythm — no centered-grid monotony
3. Handcrafted texture — subtle grain, worn edges, and organic shapes
4. Music-first hierarchy — song artwork and titles dominate the visual space

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep mahogany background: `oklch(0.18 0.04 40)` — like aged bourbon in a dimly lit room
- Warm amber accent: `oklch(0.72 0.18 65)` — the glow of a stage spotlight
- Cream text: `oklch(0.94 0.02 80)` — aged parchment, readable and warm
- Muted sage: `oklch(0.55 0.06 145)` — a nod to Oklahoma hillsides
- Signature brand color: Amber gold `#D4872A`

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Hero: Full-bleed photo with diagonal text overlay and bold title
- Songs: Masonry-style card grid with album art thumbnails
- Poems: Typographic editorial layout, left-aligned with generous margins
- Navigation: Sticky top bar that transitions from transparent to dark on scroll

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin amber horizontal rule separators with a small music note icon
2. Song cards with warm hover glow and a "Buy $1.29" CTA badge
3. Serif display font (Playfair Display) for headings, clean sans (Lato) for body

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states reveal warm amber glows and subtle scale transforms
- "Buy" buttons pulse gently to draw attention without being garish
- Smooth scroll between page sections

**Animation:**
- Cards fade-slide in from below on scroll (stagger 60ms)
- Nav transitions opacity/blur on scroll
- Button press: scale(0.97) 160ms ease-out

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (Bold, Italic) — for hero titles and section headers
- Body: Lato (Regular 400, SemiBold 600) — for descriptions and navigation
- Accent: Playfair Display Italic — for poem text

**Brand Essence:**
"Heartfelt country music crafted from real life — for listeners who feel deeply."
Personality: Warm · Authentic · Soulful

**Brand Voice:**
Headlines sound like a front-porch conversation — direct, honest, poetic.
- "Songs that carry what words alone can't hold."
- "Own a piece of the music. $1.29 a song."

**Wordmark & Logo:**
A simple guitar silhouette with a single music note, rendered in amber on dark background.
